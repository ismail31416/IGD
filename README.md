# 🎮 UniGame Dataset

The **UniGame** dataset explores the relationship between gaming habits and academic performance among students. It includes features like age, education, CGPA, sleep habits, gaming patterns, and more.

---

## 📁 Dataset Overview

### 🔍 Description

This dataset is designed to study the behavioral and academic patterns of students who engage in video gaming. It captures demographic info, academic background, and emotional/behavioral responses tied to gaming.

- **Curated by**: Hossain et al.
- **Language**: English
- **License**: [MIT](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT)

---

## 📦 Files

- `igd_data.csv` – ✅ **Processed dataset** used for modeling.
- `igd_responses_raw.csv` – 📝 **Raw dataset** collected via surveys.
- `processing_plotting_notebook.ipynb` – 📊 Notebook for data cleaning, preprocessing, and visualization.
- `IGD_ML.ipynb` – 📈 Notebook for ML validation.

## 🧾 Questionnaire (Columns Overview)

1. `What is your age?`
2. `Current educational position?`
3. `Gender?`
4. `Your current CGPA?`
5. `Your Higher Secondary School(H. SC) or A level or equivalent result?`
6. `At what age you had started playing games?`
7. `Do you play games on mobile or pc?`
8. `When you go to sleep?`
9. `Do you attend your morning class regularly?`
10. `The average time you spend playing games?`
11. `Do you play paid or non-paid games?`
12. `How many time you spend with family and friend?`
13. `How you fill when you can not play game in whole day?`
14. `How you fill to complete game level?`
15. `If you didn't finish games last level what is your feeling?`
16. `Do you fill Fatigue?`
17. `Do you play games for stress relief?`
18. `Are you wearing glasses?`
